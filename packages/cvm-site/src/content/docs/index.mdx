## What OpenCVM provides

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Regulatory filings">
    Structured ITR, DFP and FRE filings from the Comissão de Valores Mobiliários, parsed into versioned JSON.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Reference prices">
    End-of-day prices, corporate actions and dividends — sourced from B3 and reconciled against filings.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Fundamentals">
    Multi-year financial statements and derived ratios, comparable across tickers and sectors.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Open infrastructure">
    Self-hostable in a single Docker compose. SQLite + Litestream replication. No vendor lock-in.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Built for the institutions that build on it

OpenCVM is the data layer. Asset managers, research desks, fintech products and quant teams
build their own analytics, screeners and dashboards on top — through a stable, versioned API
or by self-hosting the full stack.
